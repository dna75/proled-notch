# Proled Notch

A smart panel for the notch or Dynamic Island of your Mac: now playing, radio stations, messages and calls, calendar, timer, clipboard history, AirPods and battery notices, an AirDrop shelf and its own volume bar.

**Website, download and Pro:** <https://prolednotch.com>

- Free to download, 14 days of all Pro features, then the free version keeps working.
- Pro (€5.99, one-time) is unlocked with a licence key and works on two Macs at the same time.
- macOS 13 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel. Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## Download

Get the latest disk image from <https://prolednotch.com/en/download/> or from the [Releases page](../../releases/latest). Open the `.dmg` and drag **Proled Notch** to your Applications folder.
